I am a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/), advised by Professor [Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). I graduated from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/) in June 2024.

My research focuses on **natural language processing and machine learning**, with specific interests in:
- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
